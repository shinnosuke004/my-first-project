# Midjourney 汎用プロンプト(v3)

Craft「mj汎用版P_v3」相当。背景崩れ対策版。2番目によく使う版。

## 実写系造形変換版(背景崩れ)

completely re-render this entire image in the new style.
keep the camera angle and the position of the buildings in the
frame. everything else may change: proportions, height, mass, and
the shape of every volume.
rebuild the architecture itself, not its surface. window shapes
and rhythm, edge and corner profiles, roofline details, railings,
balconies, structural members, and all materials are replaced.
the surrounding buildings are rebuilt the same way, individually,
each keeping its own position.
relight the whole scene: new light direction, new atmosphere, new
air. depth and distance should read through the air itself.
the result should look like a place from the new style,
photographed at the same camera angle. not the original place
with decoration added.

photographic realism, fine material detail.
no illustration, no flat shading, no line art, no anime.  --s 1000  --chaos 100

## イラスト系

commit fully to the rendering medium of the new style: its line
quality, its shading, its color handling, its resolution.   --s 1000  --chaos 100
